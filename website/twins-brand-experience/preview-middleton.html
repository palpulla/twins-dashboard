Local garage door service

Practical help for Middleton homeowners.

The doors here tell Middleton's story in layers. The blocks south of Century Avenue carry a lot of 1960s and 70s ranches whose original hardware is now decades past its design life, while Middleton Hills was built in the 1990s with garages tucked politely behind the houses, many on alleys, where a door that won't close is a door the whole lane notices. Out toward Bishops Bay it's new construction with tall insulated double doors and side-mount openers, a completely different parts list. We stock the truck for all three eras.

We're not a call center with a map. Twins Garage Doors is a family company run by twin brothers out of our Madison shop, and Middleton is part of the regular route. There's a good chance we've already worked on a door on your street.

Winter finds Middleton doors two ways. The wind that comes across the Pheasant Branch marsh piles snow hard against north-facing doors, and when a worn bottom seal lets meltwater creep under on a thaw day, the next hard freeze glues the door to the slab. Then the opener strains against a frozen door, and a cheap seal problem becomes a stripped gear or a bent top panel. If your door sticks on cold mornings, that's the sequence we're interrupting, and catching it at the seal stage is by far the cheapest version.

Frequently asked questions

Garage door questions, answered straight

How much does it cost to repair a garage door in Middleton?

Most spring replacements land between $575 and $1,225 for the complete job, cable repairs between $325 and $625, and minor opener repairs between $100 and $300. Those ranges come from our own completed local jobs, including 93 in Middleton over the last two years. You approve a flat quote first.

How quickly can a technician get to Middleton?

Same-day in most cases. Middleton is one of the closest cities to our Madison shop, straight up the Beltline. Call (608) 420-2377, tell us what the door is doing, and we'll give you an honest arrival window instead of a number we invented.

Do I need to replace both springs if only one is broken?

Usually yes, and it saves you money. Both springs have cycled the same number of times, so the survivor is close behind. Replacing the pair in one visit costs far less than two separate spring calls, which is part of why our $575 to $1,225 range covers the whole ticket.

How important is an insulated garage door in Middleton?

Important if the garage is attached or you work in it. An insulated Clopay door takes the edge off a Wisconsin January, protects anything stored inside, and cuts the load on rooms above the garage. Most new doors we install in Middleton are insulated steel for exactly that reason.

What are the most common garage door issues in Middleton homes?

Broken springs lead by a mile, then doors frozen to the slab in winter, then opener sensors knocked out of line. Homes from the 1960s and 70s south of Century Avenue often still carry original hardware, and steel that old fails without much warning. A $49 tune-up catches most of it early.
