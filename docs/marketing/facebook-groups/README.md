# Madison-Area Facebook Groups for Twins Garage Doors

**52 verified, active Facebook groups** in Madison WI and surrounding Dane County communities where Twins can build brand awareness, catch garage-door recommendation requests, and develop referral partnerships. Every group was opened and read in a logged-in browser on 2026-07-06; nothing here is guessed.

**Files in this folder**
- `madison-facebook-groups.csv` - the full database, all 26 columns, sortable in any spreadsheet.
- `participation-playbook.md` - how to participate without getting flagged, plus 5 ready-to-use posts.
- This README - rankings and segmented lists.

## How to read this (and what is honestly known vs. not)

Facebook shows a group's name, privacy, member count, monthly post volume, rules, and admins on the public **About** page, even for private groups. All of that was read directly and is in the database. What Facebook hides until you actually join a private group is the **post feed** - so three columns (`# recent home-service rec posts`, `garage-door posts found`, `competitor activity found`) are marked **"Verify on join"** rather than filled with guesses. The playbook explains what to log once you are in.

**8 out-of-state impostor groups were found and removed** during verification (a "Madison" in New Jersey, a "Fitchburg" in Massachusetts, two "Middleton" groups in Massachusetts and the UK, three Minnesota groups, one Indiana group). This is exactly why every group was individually opened rather than trusted from search results.

## Scoring (0-100)

Local relevance /20 + homeowner concentration /20 + recent activity /15 + recommendation-request frequency /15 + business participation allowed /10 + home-service relevance /10 + limited competitor activity /5 + referral-partnership potential /5.

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 - Join immediately:** active, local, homeowner-heavy, and allows recommendations or reasonable business posts. (10 groups)
- **Tier 2 - Join and build relationships:** great audience, but direct promotion is limited or banned; win by being helpful and catching recommendation requests. (23 groups)
- **Tier 3 - Referral networking:** B2B / Realtor / landlord / inspector / trades groups for reciprocal referrals. (6 groups)
- **Tier 4 - Monitor only:** relevant but low activity or promotion prohibited. (13 groups)
