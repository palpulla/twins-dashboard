# Twins Garage Doors — Facebook Group Participation Playbook

**Purpose:** How to show up in Madison-area Facebook groups so Twins earns trust, referrals, and repair/replacement jobs without getting flagged as a spammer or banned. Pairs with `madison-facebook-groups.csv` (the scored group database) and the tier lists in this folder.

**Golden rule:** In local groups, reputation compounds and advertising burns. Most of these groups ban or limit self-promotion. You win by being the helpful local garage-door owner people already recognize when they ask "who do you recommend," not by posting ads. Give value first, mention Twins only when it's genuinely on-topic or allowed.

---

## The three ways to participate (match to the group's tier)

1. **Answer, don't advertise.** When a homeowner asks about a stuck door, a broken spring, a noisy opener, or "who fixes garage doors," reply with a short, genuinely useful answer and a soft mention that you own Twins. This is allowed in almost every group, even ones that ban promo posts, because you are responding to a request.
2. **Be a known face.** Comment helpfully on non-garage posts occasionally (weather prep, home maintenance season reminders). Familiarity is what makes your name the first reply next time someone needs a door.
3. **Post only where allowed.** A minority of groups allow a business intro or have a weekly promo/self-promo day. Only there do you post a standalone introduction, and only on the permitted day.

## Ground rules (do not break these)

- Read each group's rules before your first post. The CSV records the ads/promo rule per group. If a rule bans promotion, never post an ad. Responding to a direct request is still fine.
- Use a personal profile to comment (people trust a person, not a logo). Business Pages are blocked from joining most personal community groups anyway.
- One helpful voice, not five. Do not have multiple people from Twins pile onto the same "who do you recommend" thread. It reads as astroturf.
- No copy-paste spam. Vary wording. Reference the specific problem the person described.
- Never promise same-day. Confirmed language is same-week service.
- Real offers only: free ($0) service call, $49 tune-up, GoodLeap financing on replacements. Do not invent discounts.
- Disclose you own the company when you recommend it. Groups and admins respect transparency and punish hidden self-promotion.
- No em-dashes, keep it plain and human.

## Cadence

- Week 1-2: join the Tier 1 and Tier 2 groups, read rules, introduce yourself only where allowed, and start answering garage-door questions as they appear.
- Ongoing: check the highest-activity groups (Dane County Neighbors, ASK Madison, the Contractors & Services group, the big Sun Prairie/community groups) a few times a week for "who do you recommend / ISO garage door" posts and reply within the hour when you can. Speed wins these.
- Tier 3 (Realtors, landlords, property managers, inspectors, other trades): these overlap the existing partner-outreach list. Use group presence to warm up before or alongside the direct emails in `twins-tier-a-ready-emails.md`.

## Five ready-to-use posts

These are written in Twins' voice. Swap the phone number to the line you want replies on (owner cell 608-515-1449, or the main 833/608 business line). Only post standalone intros (#1, #2, #5) where the group's rules allow it or on a promo day. #3 and #4 are reply templates you can use almost anywhere because they respond to a request.

### 1. General community group intro (where business intros are allowed)
Hi neighbors, I am Daniel, one of the owners of Twins Garage Doors here in the Madison area. We are a local family-run shop that does residential garage door repairs and replacements: broken springs, snapped cables, off-track doors, dead openers, keypads, and tune-ups. I hang around this group because I like helping people avoid a $600 problem when it is really a $150 one, so if your door is acting up feel free to tag me or ask. Happy to give an honest read even if the answer is "that is an easy DIY fix." We offer a free service call so a visit does not cost you anything to get a real diagnosis.

### 2. Moms / family group intro (where allowed)
Hi all, Daniel here, local dad and co-owner of Twins Garage Doors. I know a garage door is nobody's favorite thing to think about until it will not open on a school morning with everyone in the car. We handle the usual residential stuff around Madison: springs, cables, openers, off-track doors, and safety sensors (the little eyes near the floor that stop the door on a kid or a bike). If yours ever acts up, ask away in here, I am glad to help you figure out whether it is a quick fix or worth a visit. Our service call is free so you are not paying just to find out what is wrong.

### 3. Reply template — someone asks "who do you recommend for a garage door?"
Hi [name], happy to help. I own Twins Garage Doors, we are a local residential shop and this is exactly what we do. If it is a spring or cable, please do not try to fight it yourself, those are under a lot of tension and are the one repair worth calling on. Send a short video or photo of the door trying to open and I can usually tell you what is going on before we even come out. Our service call is free, and most repairs we can get to within the week. Feel free to DM me.

### 4. Reply template — someone posts a specific symptom (loud, slow, won't close, etc.)
That sounds like [likely cause: a worn roller / a spring that is losing tension / the safety sensors being out of alignment]. A couple of quick things you can check first: [one genuinely useful tip, e.g. "wipe the little sensor eyes near the floor and make sure both show a steady light, a bumped sensor is the most common no-close cause"]. If that does not do it, I own Twins Garage Doors and we can take a look. No charge for the visit to diagnose it, and I will always tell you if it is something simple you can handle yourself.

### 5. Business networking / referral group intro
Hi everyone, Daniel with Twins Garage Doors, a local residential garage door company serving Madison and the surrounding communities. We do repairs and replacements: springs, cables, openers, and full door installs with financing available. I am always happy to be the garage-door referral for your clients or customers, and glad to return the favor. If you are a Realtor, property manager, inspector, or another home-service pro and want a reliable same-week garage door contact, reach out. We keep it simple: one point of contact, photos and a clear quote before any work, and a free service call.

## What to log after you join (fills the CSV's "must be inside the group" columns)

For each group you join, note in the CSV: date of the most recent posts you see, roughly how many posts per week, whether you spot any "ISO garage door / contractor" requests in the last month, and whether a competitor (other garage door company) is already active. Those columns are marked "verify on join" in the database because Facebook hides a private group's post feed until you are a member.
