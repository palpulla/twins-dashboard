# Aman Internship Kickoff Package — Design & Content

**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Owner:** Daniel
**Hire:** Aman Kharga — Internal Operations Manager (internship phase)
**Arrangement:** ~20 hrs/week, part-time, remote. $30/hr.
**Reference role:** `Internal_Ops_Manager_Role_Description` (the senior, long-term seat)

## Purpose

Turn a broad senior role description into a focused internship starting point, while
keeping the long-term path toward Internal Operations ownership and a PE/strategic exit
(24-36 months) clear. The internship does not assume Aman can own the full senior seat
on day one. It assumes he is a sharp, limited-time student who can research, organize,
and build, and that the strongest early work plays to his finance/PE background.

## Key decisions (locked)

- **Hours/cadence:** ~20 hrs/week, part-time. Plan is milestone-based, not hours-policed.
- **First two projects:** (1) PE diligence checklist + buyer research, (2) audit and
  organize the *existing* SOPs. These map to Role Description Accountabilities #2 and #7.
- **EOD report:** on each day he works (not daily-calendar). Built as a Typeform modeled
  on Ivory's CSR EOD form.
- **SOPs already exist.** The work is audit, organize, improve, connect, operationalize.
  Not "write SOPs from scratch."
- **PE work during internship is research and drafting only. No buyer contact.**

Comp is already handled in the offer/start call; it is deliberately kept out of the kickoff
content. (For the record only: $30/hr @ 20 hrs/wk; the older Cowork IOM Role Description doc
says $25/40 and can be reconciled separately. Not part of this call.)

## 1. The 45-minute kickoff agenda

The call is about **what Aman does day to day and exactly what to start on**, not strategy or
comp. Same "say-this / [gold blanks] / cue" format. Seven blocks, 45 minutes. The doc doubles
as Aman's reference: it carries his daily loop, weekly rhythm, and a concrete week-1 task list.

| # | Block | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The frame, fast (intern now, senior later) | 3 min |
| 2 | What your day-to-day looks like | 8 min |
| 3 | Your weekly rhythm | 4 min |
| 4 | Your first tasks (the heart of the call) | 15 min |
| 5 | How you track and report | 8 min |
| 6 | What you decide vs. what needs me | 4 min |
| 7 | Gut-check and close | 3 min |

Block 1 reframes intern-vs-senior in two lines and drops the PE/NDA confidentiality note (kept
brief). Blocks 2-4 are the meat: walk the daily loop, the weekly rhythm, and the task list while
screen-sharing the Drive folder and task sheet. Block 5 hands off the daily checklist + EOD form.
Block 6 is the light decision-rights line (act on research/drafting; ask on money/vendors/outside
contact). Block 7 is the gut-check + next-step dates.

**Collaborative throughout.** The call is two-way, not a download. Each block adds an "Ask"
prompt for Aman's opinion (his first read on the role, his preferred cadence, whether the two
projects are the right starting point, what he'd reorder or add). The task list is explicitly
framed as a starting point he can reshape, so he isn't boxed into something that may not be right.

**Access + delivery commitment.** A reference section "What You'll Get After This Call" makes
explicit that Daniel provides access to every platform Aman needs (Google Workspace/Drive, CRM,
scheduling, anything a task requires; same-day on request) and sends all docs (checklist, EOD
link, role doc, task-sheet template) after the call, plus any further reports/checklists through
the day and week as needed. The daily checklist repeats the "ask and you'll have it same day" line.

### Your day-to-day (reference page in the doc)

Tools: **Google Workspace** (Drive/Docs/Sheets) + text/email. SOPs live in Drive; tasks tracked
in a Google Sheet. A typical work session: open the Internal Ops Drive folder + task sheet →
check email/texts from Daniel → pick 1-2 tasks and mark In Progress → do the work, save to Drive
named clearly → update the sheet/trackers → submit the EOD report.

### Your weekly rhythm

~20 hrs/wk on his own schedule (sends Daniel his rough working days) · **Leadership L10
Wednesdays 8:00 AM CST** · **all-team L10 Thursdays [time] CST** · weekly 30-min 1:1 · EOD
report each day worked · one improvement idea/week · Friday: skim the task sheet and update the
tracker. (NDA already signed, so the kickoff just notes confidentiality, no signing step.)

### Your first tasks, Week 1 (concrete checklist, with "add 3 of your own")

- **Setup:** accept Workspace access; create his task sheet (Task, Project, Status, Notes, Link)
  + Drive subfolders; 30-min intro call with Charles (FOM).
- **Project 1, SOP audit:** find every place SOPs/process docs live; build an inventory sheet
  (name, location, last updated, owner, format); mark current/outdated/unclear + gaps.
- **Project 2, PE diligence (research only):** research what a home-services buyer wants in
  diligence (3-4 sources, save links); draft checklist categories; list line items.
- **Wrap:** put the SOP inventory + diligence checklist v0 on one page (the week-1 deliverable);
  add at least three tasks of his own.

## 3. First-week assignment

One page, two artifacts, due end of week one:

1. **SOP inventory.** Every SOP that exists today: name, where it lives, last updated, owner.
   A simple table. The point is a complete picture of what we have.
2. **PE diligence checklist v0.** A rough outline, from his own research, of what a buyer of
   a home services / trades company expects to see in diligence. Categories and line items.
   Rough is the goal; it shows how he organizes and researches.

Keep it to ~20 hours. No system changes, no meetings to run. Inventory and research only.

## 4. 30-day internship plan (~20 hrs/week, milestone-based)

**Week 1 — Orient + inventory.** Read the role description and this kickoff doc. Get access.
Skim one recorded vendor call and shadow one CSR/DSR call if available. Build the SOP
inventory and the diligence checklist v0.
*Deliverable: SOP inventory + diligence checklist v0.*

**Week 2 — Audit pass.** Score each existing SOP: exists / outdated / not-followed / missing.
Research what buyers want in home-services diligence and turn it into a short brief.
*Deliverable: SOP audit matrix + "what buyers want" brief.*

**Week 3 — Build the trackers.** Stand up an exit-readiness tracker (item, owner, priority,
timeline). Reorganize the SOP library into a clean structure with an index. Start a
buyer-universe research file (research only, no contact).
*Deliverable: exit-readiness tracker v1 + reorganized SOP library index + buyer-universe file.*

**Week 4 — Synthesize + present.** Write a one-page "State of the Back-Office" memo for
Daniel and propose the next 30 days. Present it in the monthly 1:1.
*Deliverable: State of the Back-Office memo + proposed next-30-days.*

This ladders directly into Role Description **Phase 1 (Onboard & Listen)** and its written
"state of the operation" deliverable.

## 5. What NOT to delegate to him yet

Research and drafting on all of these is welcome. Decisions and outside contact are not.

- Any contact with potential buyers, PE firms, or M&A advisors.
- Vendor termination, renewal, or contract changes.
- Pricing changes of any kind.
- Hiring, firing, or pay decisions.
- Payroll, banking, or money-movement access and approvals.
- Signing or committing on behalf of Twins.
- Customer-facing authority as a Twins decision-maker.
- Running the weekly Level-10 meeting (defer until he has ramped; it is in the senior role,
  not the first 30 days).
- Direct, unsupervised access to sensitive financials beyond what a specific research task
  needs.

## 6. Questions Daniel should ask in the call

1. "When you read the role description, which of the priorities did you want to attack first,
   and why?" *(His application prompt. Listen for reasoning, not flattery.)*
2. "You have limited hours. If you only had five productive hours this week, what would you
   spend them on?" *(Triage instinct.)*
3. "Where have you actually used AI to save real time? Name the tool and the outcome."
4. "Walk me through how you would build a diligence checklist for a business like ours from
   scratch." *(His wheelhouse; see if he reasons from the buyer backward.)*
5. "When there is no process and no obvious owner, what do you do?" *(Ambiguity.)*
6. "How will you keep me from becoming your bottleneck?" *(Proactivity and managing up.)*
7. "What part of this feels least comfortable to you right now?" *(Honesty test.)*

## 8. Aman EOD Typeform

**Status: built and live.**
- Form: **Twins Internal Ops: End of Day Report**
- Form ID: `XcMEqQ1C`
- Live URL: https://form.typeform.com/to/XcMEqQ1C
- Workspace: GSEZQk. Theme: iFN1ISoC (same as Ivory's, for visual consistency).

**v2: router design** (revised per Daniel's feedback). Output-focused, not a diary. The
priority question routes to one tailored follow-up so the form never asks about areas he
didn't touch. Required = actionable; manager-alert on blocked + decision. ~8-9 answers on a
normal day, 3-5 min. Refs/columns updated in the edge function + `internal_ops_eod_reports`.

Fields (refs in parens):
1. Date worked (`date_work`, date, req)
2. Your name (`aman_name`, dropdown: Aman Kharga, req)
3. Hours worked today (`hours_today`, number, req)
4. What did you produce today? Paste links to docs/sheets/trackers/SOPs/folders updated
   (`produced`, long text, req)
5. Which priority did today's work mainly support? (`priority`, dropdown, req) — PE exit
   readiness · SOP/process audit or improvement · Weekly scorecard/reporting · Field-office
   coordination · Vendor/process cleanup · Bookkeeping coordination · Other
   - → if PE: `pe_area` (dropdown: data room/diligence · buyer research · financial/KPI
     readiness · SOP/process readiness · vendor/contracts cleanup · org chart/accountability ·
     systems/data cleanup · Other) + `pe_detail` (what you found/produced + links/sources)
   - → if SOP/process: `sop_detail` (what you audited/improved/organized/documented, what
     changed, where it lives, next step)
   - → any other priority: `other_detail` (what moved, where it lives, next step)
6. Is anything blocked? (`blocked`, yes/no, req) → if Yes: `blocked_detail` (what's blocked,
   what you need, urgency) *(manager alert)*
7. Do you need a decision or approval from Daniel? (`decision`, yes/no, req) → if Yes:
   `decision_detail` (what needs deciding, options, recommendation) *(manager alert)*
8. What are the top 1-3 things you'll work on next? (`next_steps`, long text, req)
9. How productive was today? (`productivity`, multiple choice: Very / Somewhat / Not, req)
   → if Somewhat/Not: `productivity_blocker` (what got in the way)
10. Anything else for Daniel? questions, ideas, flags (`anything_else`, long text, optional)

Replaced the prior v1 (separate SOP/PE gates, mood pulse, four overlapping Daniel-questions).

### Notifications / alerts (decision pending)

Ivory's form is wired to a production Supabase edge function (`csr-eod-response` on jwrpj)
that emails Daniel + Ivory via Resend, flags ACTION-NEEDED items, and writes to a
`csr_eod_reports` table, plus a reminder cron (`csr-eod-reminder`). Aman's form currently
captures responses but has **no** webhook/email wired up yet.

Options to match Ivory:
- **A. Full parallel pipeline:** new `internal-ops-eod-response` edge function (emails Daniel
  + Aman, ACTION NEEDED on blockers/decisions = Yes), optional `internal_ops_eod_reports`
  table, optional reminder cron, webhook on form `XcMEqQ1C`. Deploys to production jwrpj.
- **B. Typeform built-in self-notification:** toggle email-on-submit to Daniel in the form's
  Connect panel. No code, no production deploy. Loses the ACTION-NEEDED flagging.

Recommend A to truly match Ivory, but it touches production and sends real emails, so it is
flagged for explicit go-ahead.

## 9. Aman daily checklist

A short every-workday checklist, branded to match Ivory's docs.

**Start of day**
- Check Daniel's replies and messages from since you last worked.
- Open your project trackers (SOP audit matrix, exit-readiness tracker).
- Pick the top one or two outcomes you will move today. Write them down.

**During the day**
- Log work as you go. Keep the SOP audit matrix and exit-readiness tracker current.
- Tag every research source so it is traceable later (a buyer will ask "how do you know").
- Research and drafting only on anything PE or vendor related. No outside contact.

**End of day (each day you work)**
- Update the trackers with what changed.
- Submit your end-of-day report: https://form.typeform.com/to/XcMEqQ1C
- Note at least one improvement idea this week.

**When you have spare time (downtime playbook)**
- Advance buyer-universe research.
- Clean up and reorganize the SOP/document library.
- Draft or improve one SOP from the existing set.
- Read one diligence or trades-M&A resource and bring back a takeaway.
