# Twins Garage Doors Location Page r29 Premium Recovery

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** Approved for implementation planning
**Scope:** Location-page experience only; Rockford is the reference implementation  
**Direction:** r29 structure and brand character with a restrained premium finish

## Decision and objective

The cinematic location-page redesign is rejected. It moved too far away from the recognizable Twins Garage Doors website, allowed photography and headlines to dominate the page, introduced inconsistent alignment, and created an overlong sequence of oversized modules.

The replacement will rebuild from the r29 location-page structure. It will preserve the familiar header, navy garage-door texture, classic Twins display typeface, contained split hero, direct conversion path, and approachable family-business character. Modernization will come from disciplined proportions, a consistent grid, clearer copy, refined photo treatment, subtle motion, and better responsive behavior—not from a new visual genre.

Success means the page immediately feels like a better version of the established Twins website: recognizable, polished, local, easy to scan, and easy to act on.

## Non-negotiable recovery rules

- Use r29 as the structural and visual baseline. Do not reuse the cinematic composition.
- Keep the established Twins logo and classic rounded display typeface.
- Do not use full-bleed or viewport-dominating photography.
- Do not use dashboard, command-center, glass-panel, orbit-line, or movie-poster styling.
- Keep the page to five primary sections after the header.
- Use one shared content grid and consistent alignment anchors throughout.
- Do not say or imply that a location recently opened.
- Do not invent response times, ratings, review counts, guarantees, certifications, service areas, financing terms, or local facts.
- Preserve verified routing, booking behavior, analytics, phone data, and location configuration.
- Present and approve a live local mockup before any staging deployment.

## Design system

### Brand character

The page should feel sturdy, neighborly, competent, and current. Navy remains the dominant brand color, warm off-white provides breathing room, and gold is used for principal actions and small pieces of linework. The garage-door panel texture from r29 remains a recognizable background motif but stays low contrast.

Premium treatment is limited to sharper typography, more deliberate spacing, refined image frames, thin gold rules, cleaner transitions, polished controls, and restrained reveal motion. These details must never increase visual scale or page length.

### Grid and alignment

- Use one centered content container with a maximum width of 1180 px.
- Desktop gutters: at least 32 px. Tablet gutters: 24 px. Mobile gutters: 20 px, reducing to 16 px only at 320 px.
- All section headings, body copy, image frames, cards, and actions align to the same grid edges.
- Two-column sections use a stable 55/45 or 60/40 relationship; do not create arbitrary offsets.
- Section padding: 72–88 px desktop, 56–64 px tablet, and 44–56 px mobile.
- Body-copy measure should remain around 55–68 characters per line.

### Typography

- Preserve the existing Twins display face for principal headings.
- Keep hero headlines to a maximum of three lines on desktop and four lines on small mobile screens.
- Use responsive sizing rather than oversized fixed type. Target approximately 52–64 px desktop and 36–44 px mobile for the hero.
- Use a clean, highly legible supporting face for body copy, navigation, buttons, labels, and reviews.
- Avoid long all-caps paragraphs and excessive gold-highlighted words.

### Image rules

- Hero photography stays in a defined frame and occupies no more than about 40–44% of the desktop hero width.
- The hero image frame targets a 4:3 or 5:4 crop and a maximum visible height of approximately 460 px at large desktop widths.
- Mobile hero photography appears after the primary copy/actions, uses a compact 4:3 crop, and stays below approximately 310 px tall.
- Supporting photographs may not be taller than their paired text block at desktop widths.
- Use no more than two substantial photographs on the page: one in the hero and one in the local-proof section.
- Service imagery must use consistent small thumbnails or a coherent icon set; it must not become three competing large photographs.
- Images use controlled crops, modest corner radii, a thin navy or gold frame, and restrained shadows.

## Page architecture

### Familiar site header

Restore the r29-style header hierarchy: logo at left, familiar navigation, phone access, and a prominent quote action. The header should be clean and aligned, not embedded in a cinematic stage.

On mobile, keep a compact logo/header row and a simple menu trigger. Phone and quote access must remain easy to reach without covering content.

### 1. Contained local hero

Use the r29 split-hero idea with corrected proportions and spacing.

Left column:

- a short location eyebrow;
- a direct local service headline using the classic display face;
- one concise paragraph focused on repair, replacement, openers, and homeowner confidence;
- primary **Get a Free Quote** action;
- secondary verified phone action;
- up to three compact proof items, only when backed by existing data.

Right column:

- one contained technician, truck, or garage-door service photograph;
- thin gold/navy framing and a subtle garage-panel background relationship;
- no copy overlay, decorative glass panels, or oversized floating badges.

The hero should fit comfortably within the first desktop viewport after the header. Its purpose is to establish location, service, trust, and next action—not to function as a poster.

### 2. Compact trust strip

A slim trust strip gives customers quick verified reasons to continue.

- Trust information stays in one horizontal row on desktop and a compact two-row treatment on mobile.
- Use only verified facts already available from site or location data.
- Keep the strip visually light, with small separators or icons rather than cards.

### 3. Clear service choices

Present repair, replacement/installation, and openers as three concise service choices.

- The three service choices use equal alignment, consistent small visuals, short outcome-led descriptions, and clear text links or actions.
- Avoid large rounded cards and large service photography.
- One small twin-character cameo may appear near the outer edge of this section as a brand signature. It must not change the grid or compete with the services.

### 4. Local story and proof

Create one editorial two-column section that feels more premium without becoming oversized.

- Pair a restrained local/service photograph with concise Rockford-specific copy sourced from verified location content.
- Explain practical service coverage and customer expectations without unverified promises.
- Follow with two or three verified reviews or proof statements in a clean aligned row.
- Reviews use restrained quotation styling and thin separators rather than large card containers.

This is the page's strongest editorial moment. It supplies local confidence and visual polish without adding multiple story sections.

### 5. Final conversion stage

End with a focused navy CTA using the garage-door texture from r29.

- Keep the headline short, one supporting sentence, one primary quote action, and the verified phone action.
- Place the twin characters at the far outer edges as modest cameos. Their scale must remain secondary to the message and actions.
- On mobile, show at most one character or reduce both to small corner details if space permits; hiding them is acceptable when clarity benefits.
- Do not create another full-height hero at the bottom.

## Copy and conversion

Copy should be direct, specific, and reassuring. It must sound like a capable family-owned garage-door company, not a luxury brand manifesto or generic SEO page.

- Lead with recognizable homeowner needs: stuck doors, broken springs or cables, unreliable openers, damaged panels or tracks, unsafe movement, and replacement needs—only where supported by existing content.
- Keep most paragraphs to two or three short sentences.
- Use one dominant action label consistently: **Get a Free Quote**.
- Keep the verified phone number prominent for urgent service.
- Eliminate repeated claims, repeated introductions, and redundant service explanations.
- Do not use “recently opened” or any equivalent wording.

## Twin-character usage

The twins are brand signatures, not layout anchors.

- Use no more than two character moments across the page: one small service-section cameo and the final CTA edge treatment.
- Do not place a large full-body character in the hero.
- Characters cannot overlap headings, buttons, review text, photographs, or mobile navigation.
- Optional motion is limited to a 3–5 px float, slight entrance, or contextual gesture.
- Character assets may be reduced or hidden on narrow screens.

## Motion and interaction

Motion should make the polished structure feel responsive without drawing attention to itself.

- Allow gentle section reveals, a small button response, restrained image-frame motion, and minimal character movement.
- Do not use parallax, continuous bouncing, ambient orbiting graphics, large cinematic wipes, or autoplay video.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` must produce a complete, stable page with no missing content.
- Hover treatments cannot carry information required to understand or use the page.

## Responsive behavior

- Validate at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 px widths.
- The hero stacks copy/actions before its image on mobile.
- Service items become a clean vertical list or compact stacked blocks, not large full-screen cards.
- Reviews stack with consistent separators and no horizontal carousel dependency.
- Buttons and navigation controls have at least 44 px touch targets.
- No text, image, character, or decorative layer may create horizontal overflow.
- Mobile page length must remain materially shorter than the rejected redesign and avoid large empty gaps.

## Accessibility and performance

- Preserve semantic heading order, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and descriptive image alternatives.
- Ensure textured backgrounds do not reduce text contrast.
- Use existing optimized assets where suitable; do not add large uncompressed photography for decorative purposes.
- Reserve image dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Keep conversion controls available and readable when scripts or animation are unavailable.

## Implementation boundaries

Implement this as a deliberate rollback and rebuild of the location-page presentation. Reuse existing content sources, location configuration, routes, analytics, conversion behavior, verified business data, and suitable assets. Replace the rejected cinematic page composition rather than layering more CSS over it.

Rockford is the local reference mockup. The implementation may extend to shared location-page components only after the Rockford result is visually approved and the shared behavior remains accurate for other markets.

## Acceptance criteria

- The local page is immediately recognizable as an improved r29 Twins Garage Doors page.
- The cinematic redesign's full-bleed imagery, dark stage, dashboard motifs, oversized modules, and long card stack are absent.
- Header, hero, and subsequent sections share one disciplined alignment grid.
- Hero photography is contained and subordinate to the service message and actions.
- No supporting image is taller than its paired content at desktop widths.
- The page contains five primary sections after the header and avoids redundant modules.
- The original logo, display font, navy palette, gold accents, and garage-door texture remain recognizable.
- Twin characters appear only as restrained brand cameos and never obstruct content.
- Copy contains no “recently opened” claim or fabricated business facts.
- Quote and verified phone actions retain existing behavior.
- Mobile layouts pass the required widths with no overflow, covered content, oversized media, or excessive empty space.
- Reduced-motion mode is complete and readable.
- A live local Rockford mockup is reviewed and approved before staging deployment.

## Approval record

On 2026-07-22, the user rejected the cinematic redesign and confirmed that r29 should become the recovery baseline. The user selected a blend of the rebuilt-and-polished r29 direction and a restrained premium evolution. The user then approved the resulting direction: r29 structure and proportions, premium typography and finishing details, one editorial local-proof moment, restrained motion, compact imagery, limited twin-character cameos, and explicit prohibition of cinematic or dashboard styling.
