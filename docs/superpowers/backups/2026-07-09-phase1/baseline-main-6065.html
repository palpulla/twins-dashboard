About this door

Carriage-house style without the upkeep

Gallery® Steel gives you the vintage grooved-panel look in high-grade steel, with insulation options that turn a classic design into a comfortable, energy-smart garage.

  • R-values from 6.3 to 18.4 across single-, double-, and triple-layer construction
  • Advanced coating system protects the steel skins against the elements
  • Ultra-Grain™ finishes deliver the wood look without wood maintenance
  • Decorative hardware and specialty glass options complete the carriage-house look
  • Consistently high customer ratings for quality and performance

Worth knowing: like all steel doors, Gallery® can dent from hard impacts, and custom sizes or premium finishes add cost and lead time.

Our take: the go-to door when you want carriage-house character with steel durability.

Ask the Twins

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I maintain a steel garage door?

Lubricate the moving parts a couple of times a year and check the weather seals. We can handle it for you with a maintenance visit.

Can Gallery® doors fit a non-standard opening?

Yes. Clopay builds custom sizes. We measure your opening precisely and order the door to fit.

Can I add security features?

Yes. Pair the door with a smart LiftMaster® opener for controlled access and monitoring from your phone.

Should I install it myself?

We recommend professional installation. Steel doors are heavy and under spring tension, and a pro install protects both you and the warranty.

What does the warranty cover?

Factory coverage for materials and workmanship, with specifics by model. We will give you the exact terms before you order.
